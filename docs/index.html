About Wandergram

We're a small team obsessed with the idea that a good recommendation beats an endless scroll. Every place on this site has been picked, not algorithmically ranked.

Our story

Wandergram started in 2019 as a shared notes document between three friends who kept sending each other the same travel questions: "where should we actually go?" It grew into a small studio that curates beaches, temples, and country guides for travelers who want fewer options and better ones.

We don't accept payment for placement. Every recommendation on this site is here because someone on our team has stood in that spot, in person, and thought it was worth telling you about.
